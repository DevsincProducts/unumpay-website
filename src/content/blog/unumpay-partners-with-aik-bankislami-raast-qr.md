## What Is Raast?

Raast, meaning “direct” in Urdu, is Pakistan's first national instant payment system, developed and operated by the State Bank of Pakistan. It was launched in January 2021 with the goal of modernising Pakistan's payment infrastructure and accelerating the country's shift toward a digital economy.

At its core, Raast enables real-time, end-to-end digital payments between individuals, businesses, and government entities, at zero cost to the end user. Unlike traditional bank transfers that can take hours or days, Raast transactions settle in seconds directly into the recipient's bank account.

Raast operates through two primary modes:

<div class="bullets-list">

- **Person-to-Person (P2P):** Individuals send and receive money instantly using a Raast ID, typically a mobile number linked to a bank account. No IBAN or account number needed.
- **Person-to-Merchant (P2M):** Customers pay businesses instantly by scanning a QR code. Funds settle directly into the merchant's bank account in real time.

</div>

The P2M mode is what matters most for Shopify merchants. It removes the need for customers to enter card details, top up a wallet, or go through a multi-step checkout. The buyer scans a QR code from any Raast-enabled banking app, confirms the amount, and the payment is done.

Raast is interoperable across all participating financial institutions in Pakistan. This means a customer using HBL's mobile app can pay a merchant registered through a completely different bank, the system connects them through the central Raast infrastructure managed by the State Bank of Pakistan.

## How Raast Works for Merchants

For merchants, Raast P2M works through QR codes, either static or dynamic:

<div class="bullets-list">

- **Static QR:** A fixed QR code displayed by the merchant. The customer scans it and enters the payment amount manually. Suitable for physical shops or simple payment links.
- **Dynamic QR:** A unique QR code generated for each transaction with the amount pre-filled. The customer scans and confirms, no manual entry required. This is the format most relevant for e-commerce and Shopify checkout flows.

</div>

The payment flow for a customer is straightforward:

<li>Customer reaches the payment step on the Shopify checkout.</li>
<li>A dynamic Raast QR code is displayed for the customer to scan.</li>
<li>Customer opens their Raast-enabled banking app and scans the QR code.</li>
<li>The payment amount is pre-filled, and the customer confirms the transaction using their bank PIN.</li>
<li>Payment is processed instantly and settled directly into the merchant's bank account.</li>
<li>The order is confirmed on both the customer's screen and the merchant's Shopify dashboard.</li>

There is no card detail entry, no wallet balance required, and no redirection to a third-party page. The checkout experience is fast, familiar to any mobile banking user, and does not require the customer to have a JazzCash or Easypaisa account, just a Raast-enabled bank account.

Funds settle directly into the merchant's IBAN, making reconciliation straightforward. Each transaction generates a unique reference that can be matched against Shopify orders.

## What Is aik by BankIslami and aikPay?

aik by BankIslami is Pakistan's first digital Islamic banking platform, a mobile-first banking app built on Shariah-compliant principles. Launched by BankIslami, one of Pakistan's established Islamic banks, aik offers digital account opening, instant money transfers, bill payments, and Raast services including P2P transfers and Raast ID management.

In January 2026, aik launched aikPay, Pakistan's first Shariah-compliant Raast-enabled QR Payment Gateway. Built on the State Bank of Pakistan's Raast infrastructure, aikPay enables real-time online payments through dynamic QR codes, allowing customers to pay instantly from their bank accounts and merchants to receive payments securely through a smooth checkout experience.

The launch was supported by the State Bank of Pakistan, with SBP's Director of Digital Innovations and Settlement in attendance, reflecting the national significance of bringing Raast P2M capabilities to online merchants at scale.

aikPay is significant for two reasons:

<div class="bullets-list">

- It is the first Shariah-compliant Raast QR gateway, making it accessible to merchants and customers who specifically seek Riba-free banking options
- It brings Raast P2M to online e-commerce checkouts in a form that is compatible with third-party platform integrations, including Shopify

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is Raast?**<br />
Raast is Pakistan's first instant payment system, developed by the State Bank of Pakistan. It enables real-time, zero-cost digital payments between individuals and merchants via mobile banking apps.

**What is Raast P2M?**<br />
Raast P2M (Person-to-Merchant) is the merchant payment layer of Raast. Customers pay merchants instantly by scanning a QR code from any Raast-enabled banking app, with funds settling directly into the merchant's bank account.

**What is a Raast ID?**<br />
A Raast ID is an alias, typically a mobile number, linked to a bank account. It allows users to send and receive money without sharing their full IBAN or account number.

**What is aik by BankIslami?**<br />
Aik by BankIslami is Pakistan's first digital Islamic banking platform. It launched aikPay, Pakistan's first Shariah-compliant Raast-enabled QR Payment Gateway, in January 2026.

**How does Raast QR payment work at checkout?**<br />
A dynamic QR code is displayed at checkout. The customer scans it with their Raast-enabled banking app, confirms the pre-filled amount, and the payment settles instantly into the merchant's account.

**Do customers need a JazzCash or Easypaisa account to pay via Raast?**<br />
No. Raast works through any participating bank's mobile app. Customers only need a Raast-enabled bank account, no specific wallet is required.

**Can Pakistani Shopify merchants accept Raast QR payments?**<br />
Yes. Through UnumPay's integration with aik by BankIslami, Pakistani Shopify merchants can activate Raast QR payments at checkout alongside other payment methods.

**Is Raast free for customers?**<br />
Yes. Raast transactions are zero-cost for end users, customers pay no fees when making Raast payments.

**How does Raast compare to JazzCash and Easypaisa?**<br />
JazzCash and Easypaisa require customers to maintain a wallet balance. Raast pays directly from a bank account, is interoperable across all participating banks, and carries no transaction fees for the customer.
