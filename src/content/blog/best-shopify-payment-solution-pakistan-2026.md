If you run a Shopify store in Pakistan, finding the right payment gateway is one of the most important decisions you will make. Shopify Payments is not available in Pakistan, which means Pakistani merchants must rely on third-party payment providers to accept payments online. The good news is that in 2026, there are more local and global options than ever before — and a smarter way to manage them all. This guide breaks down what Pakistani Shopify merchants need, what online payment gateway options are available in Pakistan, and how to choose the best payment gateway setup for your store.

## Is Shopify Payments Available in Pakistan?

No. Shopify Payments is currently not available in Pakistan one of the first questions Pakistani merchants ask when setting up their Shopify store.

Shopify Payments is only supported in a limited number of countries, primarily North America, Europe, and a handful of Asia-Pacific markets. Pakistan isn’t among them.

This does not mean you cannot accept payments on Shopify in Pakistan. It simply means you need to connect a third-party payment provider to your store. Shopify fully supports this, and there is a wide range of [Shopify payments alternatives in Pakistan](/shopify-payments-alternative-in-pakistan) - from local bank gateways and mobile wallets to international processors - covering most Shopify checkout payment methods Pakistani merchants need.

## What Pakistani Shopify Merchants Actually Need

The payment landscape in Pakistan is different from Western markets. Customer behaviour, banking infrastructure, and digital adoption patterns all shape what a solid payment setup looks like for a Pakistani e-commerce store. Here is what most merchants need:

<div class="bullets-list">

- **Local payment method support:** A large portion of Pakistani customers pay using mobile wallets like JazzCash and Easypaisa, or through direct bank transfers. If your checkout does not offer these, you risk losing a significant share of orders before they are completed.
- **Card payment acceptance:**  Visa and Mastercard remain widely used, particularly among urban shoppers and customers making higher-value purchases.
- **Cash on Delivery (COD) management:** COD is still a preferred payment method across Pakistan, especially for first-time buyers and customers in smaller cities. A good setup should support COD alongside digital options.
- **Multi-gateway flexibility:** Relying on a single payment provider creates risk. If that provider goes down, your store stops accepting payments entirely.
- **Unified reporting and reconciliation:** Managing transactions across multiple gateways without a single view makes accounting unnecessarily complex. One dashboard saves significant time.
- **Local checkout trust:** Pakistani customers are more likely to complete a purchase when they see familiar, trusted payment options at checkout. Offering local payment methods directly affects conversion rates.

</div>

## Payment Gateway Options Available in Pakistan for Shopify

Pakistani merchants have access to a range of local and international online payment gateway options. Here is an overview by category:

**Local bank-based payment gateways:**
Several major Pakistani banks offer payment gateway services that integrate with Shopify through a third-party platform. These include HBL, MCB, Bank Alfalah, Meezan Bank, BOP, and Allied Bank - well suited for merchants who want card processing and bank-based payments through established local institutions.

**Mobile wallets:** 
JazzCash and Easypaisa are the two most widely used mobile wallets in Pakistan. For Pakistani Shopify merchants, offering at least one of these at checkout is increasingly essential rather than optional.

**Fintech and digital payment providers:** 
Platforms like Keenu, Abhi Pay, and Assan Pay offer modern, API-driven payment solutions built for the Pakistani market - a practical alternative for merchants looking beyond traditional bank gateways.

**International payment gateways:** 
For merchants selling cross-border, gateways like Checkout.com and Authorize.net extend your payment reach beyond Pakistan. These are particularly useful for Pakistani merchants targeting customers in the UAE, the UK, or other international markets.

If the majority of your customers are based in Pakistan, local payment methods should take priority. JazzCash, Easypaisa, and bank-based gateways will cover a large portion of your transactions and build checkout trust with local buyers.

If you are selling internationally - or planning to expand to markets like the UAE, Saudi Arabia, or the UK - you will need international gateways alongside your local options. Merchants who can serve both Pakistani customers and global buyers from a single Shopify store are positioned to grow faster.

The challenge is that integrating and managing multiple gateways individually is complex. Each requires separate setup, separate credentials, and separate reporting. This is where a payment mediation platform becomes the practical solution for most stores.

## How to Choose the Best Shopify Payment Gateway in Pakistan

When evaluating payment gateway options for your Shopify store in Pakistan, consider the following:

<div class="bullets-list">

- **Local payment method coverage:** Does the gateway or platform support JazzCash, Easypaisa, and major Pakistani bank gateways? Local coverage directly determines how well your checkout converts.
- **Ease of Shopify integration:** Some gateways require developer resources. Look for options with no-code or low-code setup, especially without a dedicated technical team.
- **Transparent pricing:** Understand the full cost - not just the headline rate, but currency conversion fees, settlement timelines, and any monthly charges. Hidden fees add up quickly at scale.
- **Reliability and uptime:** A gateway that goes down during peak hours is a serious business risk. Look for providers with strong uptime records and local support.
- **Unified reporting:**  If you plan to use more than one gateway - which is advisable - you need a single view of all transaction data. Separate dashboards create reconciliation headaches.
- **Scalability:** Choose a setup that grows with your business. If you plan to expand beyond Pakistan into regional markets, your payment infrastructure should support that without a complete rebuild.

</div>

Choosing the right third-party payment provider for Shopify in Pakistan is not about picking the most popular name - it is about matching your customer base, product category, and growth plans.
